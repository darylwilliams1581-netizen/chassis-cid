# Year 9 English — sample notes

These notes are a short original summary written for testing school mode.
They are not copied from any published textbook.

## Sentences

A complete sentence needs a subject and a verb. A fragment is missing one of those, so it cannot stand on its own.

A comma splice joins two complete sentences with only a comma. Use a full stop, a semicolon, or a joining word such as "and" or "but" instead.

## Paragraphs

A paragraph should usually stay on one idea. Start a new paragraph when the topic, the speaker, or the time clearly changes.
